AI questions

Not sure what to book? and we’ll suggest a member or a class.

Can someone help me set up Claude or other AI tools?

Yes. Members can install and set up tools like Claude Code with you on your own laptop, build custom skills and agents, or teach you to use ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini on your real work.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Most AI sessions and both classes are for people who don’t code. For Claude Code or building an app, the member types alongside you and explains each step.

What do I need for a session or class?

A laptop, and the work you want help with: your inbox, a project folder, a paper, a spreadsheet. We use your real work so you leave with something that already runs.

Which AI tools do members work with?

Each member lists their own, usually Claude, Claude Code, ChatGPT and Gemini, plus automation tools for agents. Ask in your request if you need a specific one.

What are group classes?

Small live classes, usually 8 to 10 people, run by one member on a set day and time. You pay per seat. They’re a cheaper way to learn things like AI basics or prompting with others.
