Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Valedictor is not open yet. Right now the only information we collect is what you type into the request form. This page explains what that is, who handles it and how to have it deleted.

Who we are

Valedictor is run by its founders, Vansh Aggarwal and Maud Bismuth, in Boston, Massachusetts. For anything on this page, email valedictor2026@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send a requestYour name, email address, whether you need help or want to be a member, your institution and field if you apply as a member, and anything you write in the message box.
When you visit the siteWe don’t use cookies, analytics or ad trackers. Like any website, the services below see basic technical details such as your IP address and browser when they deliver the page.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your request and, when we open, to match you with a member or review your application.
  • To tell you when Valedictor opens in Boston. You can ask us to stop at any time.

We don’t sell your information and we don’t share it with advertisers.

Services that handle it

GitHub PagesHosts this website.
FormSubmitPasses the request form to our inbox by email.
GoogleGmail is where requests arrive. Google Fonts supplies the typeface on this site.

These services may process data in the United States and elsewhere, under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep requests while we’re preparing to open and for as long as they’re useful for matching or membership. If you’d like yours deleted sooner, ask and we’ll delete it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete what you sent us, or to stop hearing from us. Email valedictor2026@gmail.com from the address you used and we’ll handle it within 30 days.

Age

Valedictor isn’t for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you’re under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before you send a request.

Changes

When we open bookings, accounts and payments, we’ll update this policy before those features go live and change the date at the top.
