Questions

Who can be a member?

Students, alumni and faculty of top institutions, plus people formally affiliated with them. We start with Harvard, MIT and Babson, then BU, Northeastern and Tufts.

How do you check members?

Every member is verified with their institution before their first booking, for example through their institution email. We also read each application ourselves.

How much does a session cost?

Each member sets their own prices. You see the full price for the length you choose before you book, and payment is only released to the member after the session happens.

Where do sessions take place?

On Valedictor, in your browser. If you both agree, the session is recorded and you each get a transcript and a short summary.

When can I book?

We’re opening in Boston first. Request access and we’ll email you when your place is ready.

Is Valedictor part of Harvard, MIT or Babson?

No. Institution names show where members study, teach or graduated. Valedictor isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.
