Questions

Who can be a member?

Students, alumni and faculty of the world's top institutions, plus people formally affiliated with them. We verify every member before they can be booked.

How much does a session cost?

Members set their own prices. Quick sessions with students start around $15 for 15 minutes; alumni and faculty usually charge more.

Are sessions recorded?

Only with both people's consent, given before the call starts. Recordings and transcripts are private to the two of you and stored in your accounts.

What if a session doesn't happen?

Your payment is held until the session is complete. If it doesn't take place, you get your money back.

Where are you starting?

Boston, with Harvard, MIT and Babson, then more institutions and more cities.
