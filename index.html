What it is

Some questions only a handful of people can answer well. The one who took the class, wrote the paper, or already got the offer you want.

Valedictor finds that person and books you time with them.

We’re starting with Harvard, MIT and Babson. Everyone who takes a booking has been checked against their institution first.

For example
  • Read my pitch deck before Thursday’s meeting30 min
  • Mock interview for a consulting final round1 hr
  • Get me unstuck on this proof15 min
  • Is my research question worth a PhD?45 min
  • Go through my go-to-market plan line by line2 hr

Each member sets their own rate.

How it works
  1. Tell us what you need help with.A sentence is enough. Our guide asks the rest.
  2. We suggest a few people who have done it.You pick one and a time that suits you both.
  3. You meet on Valedictor.Recorded only if you both agree. Afterwards you each get the transcript and a short summary.
